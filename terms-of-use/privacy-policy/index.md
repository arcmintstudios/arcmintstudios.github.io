# Privacy Policy 

Last updated: July 25, 2026

<span class="smart-name">Smart</span> <span class="rupee-name">Rupee</span> ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

## 1. 100% On-Device Data Processing
<span class="smart-name">Smart</span> <span class="rupee-name">Rupee</span> is designed with a "Privacy First" architecture. All your financial data, including:
- Bank SMS messages
- Transaction history
- Account balances
- Financial goals and budgets
- Asset details (Gold, Investments, etc.)

**REMAINS ON YOUR DEVICE AT ALL TIMES.** We do not have servers that store your financial information. There is no cloud sync feature that transmits your sensitive financial data to us or any third party.

## 2. Permissions We Request
To provide our services, the app requests the following permissions:
- **READ_SMS / RECEIVE_SMS**: To automatically detect and categorize your financial transactions. This processing happens entirely locally using on-device regular expressions and machine learning models.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: To send you instant alerts about new transactions and daily summaries.
- **SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW**: To show local transaction pop-ups over other apps.
- **BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT**: To secure your app data locally.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use a limited number of third-party services for specific non-sensitive tasks:
- **mfapi.in**: To download Mutual Fund NAV data and scheme lists. We only send the scheme code to fetch the price; no personal or holding data is ever sent.

## 4. Data Security
Since all data is stored in a local SQLite database, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend:
- Enabling the "App Lock" feature within <span class="smart-name">Smart</span> <span class="rupee-name">Rupee</span> (PIN, Password, or Biometric).
- Keeping your device OS updated.
- Being cautious when creating manual backups, as backup files (`.db`) are not encrypted by default.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the version in the app.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository issues.
