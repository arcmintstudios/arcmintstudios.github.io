Privacy Policy — KidGuard

Last updated: June 2026  |  Developer: ArcMint Studios

KidGuard ("App") is developed by ArcMint Studios ("we", "Developer"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

1. Data We Collect

KidGuard does not collect personal data and does not transmit any user data to our servers. All app data is stored locally on your devices.

Data stored on your phone:

  • List of apps installed on the paired TV (package names and labels only)
  • Lock/unlock state per app
  • Bedtime schedule settings you create
  • Daily time limit settings you configure
  • Emergency unlock code (stored encrypted using AES-256)
  • TV usage logs (shown in the Insights screen)
  • Pro subscription status (stored encrypted locally)

Data stored on your TV:

  • List of locked app package names received from your phone
  • Bedtime schedule window
  • Daily time limit values per app

2. How Data Is Used

All data is used solely to provide the app's features — controlling TV app access from your phone. No data is shared with third parties, advertisers, or analytics services.

3. Network Usage

KidGuard communicates between your phone and TV over your local Wi-Fi network only. The app connects to the internet only for the following purposes:

  • Google Play Billing — to process in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Google Play In-App Review — to show a rating prompt (optional; no data stored by us)

No other internet connections are made by the app.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

KidGuard offers optional paid subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play Billing. All payment transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not store any payment or billing information. Your subscription status is stored locally on your device.

5. Camera, Microphone, Location

KidGuard does not access your camera, microphone, or location at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

KidGuard is a parental control tool designed for use by parents and guardians. The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from children. The KidGuard TV app (installed on the TV) does not collect or store any child data.

7. Data Deletion

Uninstalling KidGuard from your phone or TV removes all locally stored data. There is no cloud backup. To cancel a subscription, use Google Play's subscription management settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be available within the app and on the app store listing. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, please contact us at: arcmintstudios@gmail.com
