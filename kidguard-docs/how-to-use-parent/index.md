# How to Use KidGuard Parent

KidGuard Parent is the app you install on your own phone. It pairs with the free **KidGuard TV** app on your kid's Android TV, and gives you control over what's watched and for how long — without sitting next to the TV yourself.

## 1. Getting Started
- Install **KidGuard TV** on the Android TV first, and open it.
- Open KidGuard Parent, tap **Pair Device**, and follow the on-screen steps — your phone finds the TV automatically over your home Wi-Fi.
- Once paired, you'll see a 4-digit PIN on the Dashboard. Keep your phone nearby — you'll need this PIN on the TV side to unlock or change settings there directly.

## 2. The Dashboard
- The top card shows whether the TV is **locked** or **unlocked**, with a one-tap toggle.
- **Pause TV** locks everything immediately and resumes automatically after a time you choose (15 min up to 1.5 hours) — handy for "five more minutes" without setting up a schedule.
- Your **Emergency PIN** is shown here (tap the eye icon to reveal it) — this is what you'd enter directly on the TV if you're locked out and can't reach this phone.
- **TV Apps** lists everything installed on the TV — toggle any app's lock switch individually, without touching the others.

## 3. Schedules
- Go to **Schedules** from Quick Actions to create recurring lock windows — e.g. "School Hours" (weekdays, 8 AM–4 PM).
- Pick the days, start/end time, and whether to block all apps or just specific ones during that window.
- Multiple schedules can run side by side — school hours, homework time, and bedtime don't need to be the same rule.

## 4. Daily Time Limits
- Go to **Time Limits** to set a per-app daily cap, in 15-minute steps.
- Once an app hits its limit, the TV locks it automatically for the rest of the day, resetting at midnight.
- Need to make an exception just once? Use **Give bonus time** on that app to grant extra minutes on the spot, without changing the daily limit itself.

## 5. Bedtime
- A dedicated automatic lock/unlock window, separate from regular schedules — set a lock time and an unlock time, and it repeats every night without you touching the app.

## 6. Insights
- The **Insights** tab shows daily and weekly screen time, broken down by app, so you can see what's actually being watched or played — not just enforce limits blind.

## 7. Account & Settings
- **Paired Devices** — see every phone approved to control this TV, and remove ones that shouldn't have access anymore.
- **Emergency PIN** — your TV recovery code, separate from the rotating 4-digit PIN, for when the TV can't reach this phone at all.
- Optionally sign in with Google or email so your Pro status and trial stay in sync if you reinstall the app or switch to a new phone — signing in is not required to use KidGuard.
- Sign out, or unpair this phone from the TV, from the same screen.

## 8. KidGuard Pro
- New installs get a **7-day free trial** with full access, no card required.
- **Pro** unlocks Bedtime schedules and Daily Time Limits; the free tier covers manual lock/unlock, Pause TV, and per-app locking.
- Choose Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly billing, or a one-time Lifetime purchase — all through Google Play Billing.
- Manage or cancel a subscription any time via Google Play → Subscriptions.
