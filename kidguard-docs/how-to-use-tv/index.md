# How to Use KidGuard TV

KidGuard TV is the free companion app installed on your kid's Android TV. It doesn't need a Google sign-in or any setup of its own — everything is configured from the **KidGuard Parent** app on your phone.

## 1. Installation & Pairing
- Install KidGuard TV from the Play Store on the Android TV, and open it.
- On your phone, open KidGuard Parent and tap **Pair Device** — it finds the TV automatically over your home Wi-Fi and pairs in a few seconds.
- Once paired, the TV enforces whatever locks, schedules, and time limits you set from the phone — you won't need to touch the TV remote for day-to-day changes.

## 2. What Your Kid Sees
- A locked app shows a lock screen with a 4-digit PIN entry — the current PIN is visible on your phone's Dashboard at all times, and rotates automatically every 5 minutes for security.
- Apps you haven't locked open normally, exactly as before KidGuard was installed.

## 3. Entering the PIN Directly on the TV
- If you're at the TV and want to unlock something without reaching for your phone, enter the 4-digit PIN shown on your phone's Dashboard using the TV remote.
- Your **Emergency PIN** (a separate recovery code from Account & Settings on the phone) also works here if the phone is unreachable.

## 4. Recovering a TV That's Lost Its Pairing
- If the paired phone is ever lost or the TV needs to be reconnected to a different phone, hold the remote's **Back** button for about 10 seconds on the PIN entry screen. This brings up a confirmation dialog to reset the TV's pairing, clearing the current PIN and approved-phone list so a new phone can pair with it.
- This is a deliberate, on-screen action — nothing about KidGuard runs hidden or without a visible indication that it's active.

## 5. What KidGuard TV Doesn't Do
- It doesn't require a Google account or any sign-in on the TV itself.
- It doesn't see or record what's actually playing — only which apps are open and for how long, so the Parent app can show usage insights and enforce time limits.
- It has no internet dependency for its core locking behavior — the phone and TV talk to each other over your local Wi-Fi network only.
