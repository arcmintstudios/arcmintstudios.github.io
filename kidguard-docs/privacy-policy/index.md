# Privacy Policy — KidGuard

Last updated: September 2026

KidGuard ("App") is developed by ArcMint Studios ("we", "our", or "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user. It covers both the **KidGuard Parent** app and the **KidGuard TV** app.

## 1. Data Stored Only On Your Devices
The following never leaves your phone or TV:
- List of apps installed on the paired TV (package names and labels only)
- Lock/unlock state per app
- Bedtime schedule settings you create
- Daily time limit settings you configure
- Emergency unlock code (stored encrypted using AES-256)
- TV usage logs (shown in the Insights screen)

## 2. Data We Collect Through Firebase
KidGuard uses Firebase, a Google service, to keep your trial and Pro status accurate across reinstalls and devices, and to let you sign in. This means the following is sent to our Firebase project:
- Your email address, if you choose to sign in with one — or an anonymous account identifier if you don't
- Your TV's device identifier, its trial start/end dates, and a cryptographic signature verifying them — sent when your phone registers a TV, specifically so that clearing app data or reinstalling on the TV can't be used to repeatedly reset a free trial
- Your Pro subscription status, so it stays consistent if you sign in on a new phone

We do not collect or transmit what's playing on the TV, browsing history, or any viewing content — only the account and entitlement data listed above.

## 3. In-App Purchases and Advertising
KidGuard offers optional paid subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play Billing. All payment transactions are handled entirely by Google Play — we do not see or store any payment or billing information. Free-tier users are shown banner ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising ID and ad-interaction data under Google's own privacy policy. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

## 4. Network Usage
KidGuard communicates between your phone and TV over your local Wi-Fi network only, using an encrypted (AES-256-GCM) connection — there is no cloud server sitting between your phone and your TV for day-to-day control. Separately, the app connects to the internet for:
- **Firebase** — account sign-in and trial/entitlement sync (see Section 2)
- **Google Play Billing** — to process in-app purchases and subscriptions
- **Google AdMob** — to serve banner ads to free-tier users
- **Google Play In-App Review** — to show a rating prompt (optional; no data stored by us)

## 5. Camera, Microphone, Location
KidGuard does not access your camera, microphone, or location at any time.

## 6. Children's Privacy
KidGuard is a parental control tool designed for use by parents and guardians — children are not expected to install, sign into, or operate the app. It does not knowingly collect information from children. The KidGuard TV app (installed on the TV) does not collect or store any child-specific data.

## 7. Data Deletion
Uninstalling KidGuard from your phone or TV removes everything stored locally on that device. Your Firebase account data and your TV's entitlement record are not automatically deleted on uninstall, since they exist specifically to prevent trial abuse across reinstalls. To request deletion of this data, contact us using the email below.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be available within the app and on the app store listing. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
