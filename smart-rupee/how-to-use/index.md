# How to Usage Guide

<span class="smart-name">Smart</span> <span class="rupee-name">Rupee</span> is your personal finance companion that automatically tracks your spending, helps you save for goals, and manages your entire portfolio in one place.

## 1. Getting Started
- **Permissions**: When you first open the app, allow **SMS Permissions**. This is required for the app to "read" bank messages and record your transactions automatically.
- **App Lock**: Go to Settings > Security to set a PIN or Fingerprint to keep your financial data private.

## 2. Automatic Expense Tracking
- Every time you spend money (UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card) and receive a bank SMS, <span class="smart-name">Smart</span> <span class="rupee-name">Rupee</span> will detect it.
- **Instant Alerts**: A small card will appear on your screen when a transaction is detected. You can quickly change the category (e.g., from 'Others' to 'Food') right there.
- **Manual Entry**: If you spend cash, use the '+' button on the Home screen to add it manually.

## 3. Managing Your Portfolio (Assets)
Go to the **Portfolio** tab to track what you own:
- **Gold**: Add your gold ornaments or coins. The app calculates their value based on weight.
- **Investments**: Track Mutual Funds, Stocks, and Fixed Deposits.
- **Insurance**: Keep a list of your Life, Health, and Term insurance policies so you never miss a renewal.
- **Property**: Record your real estate holdings.

## 4. Setting Budgets
- Go to the **Planning** tab > **Budget**.
- Set monthly limits for categories like 'Groceries', 'Rent', or 'Entertainment'.
- The app will warn you if you are about to exceed your limit.

## 5. Saving for Goals
- Want to buy a car or save for a vacation? Go to **Planning** > **Goals**.
- Add your target amount and date.
- The app will tell you how much you need to save every month (SIP) to reach that goal on time.

## 6. Net Worth Dashboard
- The **Wealth** tab shows your total Net Worth (Assets minus Liabilities/Loans).
- See beautiful charts of how your wealth is growing month by month.

## 7. Data & Privacy
- **100% On-Device**: Your bank data never leaves your phone. We have no servers and cannot see your balance.
- **Backup**: Regularly use Settings > Backup to save a file of your data. If you change phones, use 'Restore' to get your data back.
